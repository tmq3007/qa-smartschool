## 📏 KÍCH THƯỚC CHI TIẾT

### Metal Clamp System (Zones 8-11)

```
Zone 8: Metal Clamp Body
├─ Outer Diameter: 12mm
├─ Inner Diameter: 8mm (fits pencil Ø7mm)
├─ Height: 6mm
├─ Split Gap: 0.3mm
├─ Rivets: Ø1.6mm × 6mm (2 pieces)
└─ Material: Aluminum/Steel

Zone 9: Adjustment Screw
├─ Shaft: Ø3mm × 8mm length
├─ Head: Ø5mm × 2mm thickness
├─ Knurl Depth: 0.2mm (alternating)
└─ Material: Steel

Zone 10: Spring Mechanism
├─ Wire Radius: 0.5mm (Ø1mm wire)
├─ Spring Radius: 3mm (Ø6mm outer)
├─ Length: 5mm
├─ Coils: 8 turns
└─ Material: Steel spring

Zone 11: Grip Pads
├─ Arc Radius: 4.5mm
├─ Arc Angle: 60° per pad
├─ Width: 4mm
├─ Thickness: 1mm
└─ Material: Rubber
```

### Center Screw (Zone 22)

```
Zone 22: Center Screw
├─ Shaft: Ø2mm × 5mm length

│   ├─ Horizontal: 1.8mm × 0.3mm × 0.75mm deep
│   └─ Vertical: 1.8mm × 0.3mm × 0.75mm deep
```
